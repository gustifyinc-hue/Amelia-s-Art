my little story

Hi, I'm Amelia

I'm 16 years old and I make paper-cut lamps. I was born with Down syndrome, but that never stopped me from creating beautiful art and sharing it with other people. I started cutting paper lamps at my kitchen table and never really stopped — and every order is one I pack myself, with a lot of love tucked inside each one.

— Amelia

Questions, answered

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship worldwide with free shipping. Most arrive within 2–4 weeks depending on where you are — I'll always email you a tracking link once it's on the way.

How is the lamp powered?

Every lamp is USB-powered with a low-heat LED, so it's safe to leave glowing all evening. Plug it into any USB port, adapter, or power bank.

What if I don't love it?

You're covered by my 30-day happiness promise. If something's wrong or it arrives damaged, email me and I'll sort out a replacement or refund — no drama.

Can I gift it?

Absolutely — these make lovely gifts. Just enter your recipient's address at checkout and I'll send it straight to them.
